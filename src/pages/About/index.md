I am a ScienceIT Consultant at the [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://www.lbl.gov/) in Berkeley, CA.

Previously, I was an Assistant Professor in the [Physics Department](https://www.plattsburgh.edu/academics/schools/arts-sciences/physics/) at [SUNY Plattsburgh](https://www.plattsburgh.edu/). My research area was cosmology. My primary research interests in cosmology revolved around making use of the cosmic microwave background and galaxy survey data to study early universe cosmology.

I did my PhD at the Pennsylvania State University, University Park and worked as a postdoctoral research fellow at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor.
